Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Cookie Policy describes the Google tags currently present in the site code and how browser and service settings affect cookies. A consent choice is not inferred from continued browsing.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site (or third-party services) to recognize your browser and remember certain information. Cookies do not contain executable code and cannot access other files on your device.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They do not store personally identifiable information and cannot be disabled without affecting core site functionality. At this time, we do not use any strictly necessary session cookies beyond what your browser handles natively.

Analytics Cookies

The site currently loads a Google Analytics 4 tag. Google says its web tags may use first-party cookies to distinguish users and sessions; these cookies should not be described as anonymous. The exact events and retention depend on Google Analytics property and tag settings.

Google documents _ga and _ga_<container-id> as GA4 cookies, with a default two-year expiration that may be shortened by browser limits or changed through tag settings. Aggregated reports do not mean the cookie-based signals are anonymous.

Advertising Cookies

The Google AdSense script is currently included in the site code. If ads are served, Google and advertising partners may process information and use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery and measurement. The exact behavior depends on approval, account settings, user choices, location, and the participating ad services.

Functionality Cookies

The site code reviewed does not currently include a feature that stores theme or region preferences in cookies.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on this Site are set by third-party services we use. We do not control these cookies. Common third-party cookie sources may include:

  • Google Analytics — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — Google Advertising Policies
  • External sites linked from this site — their cookie practices are governed by their own policies

4. How to Control Cookies

You have several options to control or delete cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings menu. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Advertising opt-out: Visit optout.aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org to opt out of interest-based advertising.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of this website or third-party services embedded within it.

5. Cookie Consent

The sampled public pages showed no visible cookie-choice interface, and the site’s static page code does not contain one. AdSense can also display account-configured privacy messages, which were not verified here. Do not treat continued browsing as consent. Before serving personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, Google requires publishers to use a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. The consent setup and tag behavior remain to be confirmed.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We will update the date above when we revise this policy. Continued browsing does not by itself indicate a choice to allow optional cookies or advertising technologies.
