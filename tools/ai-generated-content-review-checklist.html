AI-Generated Content Review Checklist

Quality Review
Free Download • DOCX & PDF

Overview

This checklist helps a person compare AI-assisted draft content with cited sources, approved records or procedures, and local review rules. It can organize questions about accuracy, references, and traceability, but it cannot guarantee that content is correct or authorize its use in a controlled workflow.

Who Is This For?

  • QA Document Controllers
  • Technical Writers
  • Regulatory Affairs professionals
  • Quality Management System owners
  • Subject Matter Experts reviewing AI outputs

Use Cases

  • Reviewing AI-drafted SOPs and work instructions
  • Reviewing AI-generated draft entries under the site’s approved batch-record procedures
  • Reviewing draft regulatory-submission content before authorized approval
  • Quality review of AI-created training materials
  • Verification of AI-generated reports and summaries

Download

The template is available in both editable Word format and PDF for printing or reference.

Key Benefits

  • Review structure: Groups checks for a separate authorized review and approval; the form does not create or replace a site process.
  • GMP-related prompts: Helps a reviewer consider local documentation controls; it does not decide whether requirements are met.
  • Source checking: Prompts a reviewer to compare content with evidence; it cannot eliminate false or unsupported output.
  • Review record: May document what a person checked if retained under local procedures; it does not make content inspection-ready.
  • Documentation prompts: Includes checkpoints that can be adapted to local Good Documentation Practice procedures.

What the Template Covers

  • Content accuracy and factual verification
  • checking regulatory terminology against source material
  • reviewing against applicable site documentation standards
  • Version control and traceability
  • approval workflow questions for adaptation to local procedures
  • AI confidence and limitations disclosure
