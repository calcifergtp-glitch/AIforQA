Regulatory Reference Library

Start with the official source. This guide groups key documents for pharmaceutical QA, GMP, computerized systems, risk management, and AI, and labels what each document is.

How to use this page: A regulation can create binding requirements. Guidance and reflection papers explain an agency’s position or recommendations but usually do not create new legal duties by themselves. ICH guidelines need to be checked against the rules adopted in your region.

Sources checked November 27, 2026. Confirm the current version and local applicability before relying on a document. Educational reference only; not legal or regulatory advice.

U.S. regulations

Binding requirements in the United States. Check the rule and its applicability to the specific record, product, and process.

U.S. regulationBinding rule
21 CFR Part 11 — Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures

Requirements for electronic records and electronic signatures when FDA rules require records or signatures to be maintained or submitted electronically.

Scope: FDA-regulated records and signatures when Part 11 applies; read together with the underlying “predicate” regulations.

21 CFR Part 211 — Drug CGMP

Current good manufacturing practice requirements for finished pharmaceuticals, including quality-unit responsibilities, records, production controls, and laboratory controls.

Scope: Finished drug products manufactured for the U.S. market.

GMP and computerized-system guidance

Official agency guidance explains regulators’ current thinking. FDA guidance is generally nonbinding; the underlying regulations still apply.

FDA guidanceFinal; nonbinding recommendations
Data Integrity and Compliance With Drug CGMP: Questions and Answers

FDA’s answers on data integrity expectations under drug CGMP, including records, audit trails, metadata, and controls.

Scope: Drug CGMP records and systems; use with the applicable regulations.

Open official source →
FDA guidanceFinal; nonbinding recommendations
Part 11, Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures — Scope and Application

FDA’s interpretation of Part 11’s scope and application. The guidance describes enforcement discretion for specified provisions; it does not remove Part 11 or underlying recordkeeping duties.

Scope: FDA-regulated electronic records and signatures. Published September 2003; check for newer agency materials.

Open official source →
EU GMP guidanceCurrent EudraLex listing: January 2011 revision
EudraLex Volume 4, Annex 11 — Computerised Systems

EU GMP guidance on computerized systems, including lifecycle controls, validation, security, data, and change management.

Scope: Medicinal products in the EU GMP framework. The Commission’s Volume 4 page lists Annex 11 as revision January 2011.

EudraLex Volume 4, Annex 1 — Sterile Medicinal Products

EU GMP guidance for sterile manufacturing, including contamination-control strategy and environmental monitoring.

Scope: Sterile medicinal product manufacture. The Commission lists Annex 1 as fully applicable since 25 August 2024.

AI-specific regulatory sources

These documents discuss AI in medicines. Their scope and legal weight differ, so they should not be treated as universal AI rules for GMP systems.

EMA reflection paperFinal reflection paper; not a regulation
Reflection Paper on the Use of AI in the Medicinal Product Lifecycle

EMA principles relevant to AI and machine learning across the medicines lifecycle, from discovery through post-authorisation.

Scope: Medicinal products in the EU; current version first published 30 September 2024.

FDA discussion paperDiscussion paper; not a regulation or final guidance
Using AI and Machine Learning in the Development of Drug and Biological Products

FDA’s discussion of considerations for AI and machine learning used in drug and biological product development.

Scope: Drug and biological product development. It does not establish a general rule for AI used in GMP operations.

Quality-system and risk-management guidelines

ICH guidelines support harmonized approaches. Their legal effect depends on adoption by the relevant regulator or region.

ICH guidelineStep 4 guideline; check regional adoption
ICH Q9(R1) — Quality Risk Management

A framework for assessing, controlling, communicating, and reviewing risks to pharmaceutical product quality across the lifecycle.

Scope: ICH Step 4 adopted 18 January 2023; implementation status can vary by region.

Open official source →
ICH guidelineStep 4 guideline; check regional adoption
ICH Q10 — Pharmaceutical Quality System

A model for an effective pharmaceutical quality system across development and manufacturing, including CAPA, change management, process performance, and management review.

Scope: ICH Step 4 finalised June 2008; implementation status can vary by region.

Related source with limited scope

Useful background for software assurance, but its scope should not be stretched beyond what FDA says it covers.

FDA guidanceFinal; nonbinding recommendations
Computer Software Assurance for Production and Quality Management System Software

A risk-based approach to assurance for software used in medical-device production and quality-management systems. FDA’s February 2026 document supersedes its September 2025 version.

Scope: Primarily medical-device production and QMS software; do not treat it as a general drug-GMP validation rule.
