Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI for Pharmaceutical QA ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit this website. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

This site currently has no newsletter sign-up, account registration, or contact form. The About page links to GitHub Issues for questions and corrections. If you choose to post there, GitHub processes information under its own terms and privacy policy; do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, we may automatically collect certain technical information when you visit, including:

  • IP address and general location (country or region)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source
  • Device type and operating system

A Google Analytics 4 tag is present on the site. Google documents that its web tags can use first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_ to distinguish users and sessions. Aggregated reports do not mean that all underlying data is anonymous; the exact data collected and retention depend on tag and property settings.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience on this site. Please see our Cookie Policy for full details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve the website
  • Understand how visitors use the site (analytics)
  • Respond to questions and corrections submitted through the GitHub Issues link on the About page
  • Serve relevant advertising (if ads are enabled)
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Affiliate Links and Third-Party Services

This site may contain affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites and recommend reviewing their privacy policies before submitting personal information.

We may use third-party services including but not limited to:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense — advertising technology; its script is present on the site, while actual ad delivery depends on account approval and settings
  • GitHub — issue hosting for the contact route linked from the About page

4. Data Sharing

Google Analytics and Google AdSense may process information when their tags are active, under the settings for those services. Contact messages posted through GitHub Issues are handled by GitHub. We may also disclose information when required by law. Privacy rights and choices can vary by location; this page does not claim that continued browsing is consent to optional tracking or advertising.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including rights to access, correct, or delete it. For questions about this site’s data practices, use the GitHub Issues link on the About page, but do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Analytics data is retained according to the settings in each analytics tool.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We will update the date above when we revise this policy. Continued browsing does not by itself indicate a choice to allow optional analytics or advertising technologies.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the contact information on our About page.
