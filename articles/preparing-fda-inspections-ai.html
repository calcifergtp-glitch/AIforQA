Preparing for FDA Inspections with AI

Compliance
2026-03-14 • 3 min read
Inspection Readiness FDA Audit Trail Review CAPA Data Integrity Compliance

Inspection readiness is everyday recordkeeping

Being ready for an FDA inspection means the company can explain how its quality system works and promptly show the records that support its decisions. AI may help locate documents, build an index, or summarize a set of records. It cannot make a missing record complete or turn an unsupported explanation into evidence.

FDA says CGMP records needed to demonstrate compliance must be retained so they are available for inspection. Keep the official records in a system the company can access and retrieve, and make sure the people responsible for the process know how to find them.

Prepare a clear path to the evidence

  • Identify current procedures, batch records, laboratory records, investigations, CAPAs, training, and equipment records relevant to the area.
  • Confirm each record is complete, approved where required, readable, and linked to the right product, lot, system, or event.
  • Check that the record can be retrieved in a usable format and that required metadata or audit trails are available.
  • Assign a knowledgeable owner for each subject area and a person to track requests and responses.
  • Use AI, if approved, to find records or draft an index; have staff compare every result with the controlled source.

Practice the retrieval process with realistic requests. Note what was hard to locate and fix the underlying filing, access, or training issue. Do not create or alter records after the fact to make a file look complete. If something is missing or incorrect, follow the normal deviation or record-correction process and be transparent.

Keep roles and communication clear

Staff should answer from the records and their knowledge, distinguish facts from interpretation, and ask for clarification when needed. AI-generated talking points should never be treated as verified answers. Quality and regulatory leads can coordinate the response, but each record owner remains responsible for providing accurate information.

Key takeaway

Use AI to reduce the time spent searching and organizing. Readiness still depends on reliable records, trained people, and honest access to the evidence.
