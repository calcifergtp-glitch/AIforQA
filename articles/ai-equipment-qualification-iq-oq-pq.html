AI for Equipment Qualification (IQ, OQ, PQ)

GxP & Validation
2026-03-14 • 3 min read
Validation GxP GMP Risk Assessment Computer System Validation

Qualification checks whether equipment is fit for its job

Equipment used in drug manufacturing must be suitable for its intended use and maintained under the applicable CGMP requirements. Many companies organize qualification evidence into installation, operational, and performance activities—often called IQ, OQ, and PQ. The exact protocol depends on the equipment, the process, the risks, and the company’s approved procedures.

AI can help organize specifications, draft a protocol outline, compare a test record with an approved acceptance criterion, or flag missing information. It cannot prove that equipment was installed correctly or that a test actually passed.

Keep the evidence tied to the real equipment

  • Installation: compare the installed model, serial number, parts, utilities, and configuration with approved specifications.
  • Operation: run approved tests under defined conditions and record actual observations and measurements.
  • Performance: show the equipment works for its intended use in the actual process, where the qualification plan requires it.

AI may assist with drafting checklists from approved requirements, but a qualified person should verify every item against the current source and installed equipment. Use actual test results, calibrated instruments, dates, identities, and deviations. Never let a model invent a value, backfill an observation, or mark an acceptance criterion passed without evidence.

Control changes after qualification

Changes to equipment, software, utilities, recipes, or intended use may affect the qualified state. Route them through change control, assess their impact, and decide whether additional testing is needed. Keep the approved protocol, completed results, deviations, approvals, and maintenance history retrievable.

If AI is used inside the equipment or in a connected quality workflow, separately assess and control that software for its intended use. A qualified machine does not automatically validate an AI feature or the records it creates.

Key takeaway

Use AI to help prepare and check qualification paperwork. Keep the test conditions, measurements, acceptance decisions, and approvals grounded in verified evidence from the actual equipment.
