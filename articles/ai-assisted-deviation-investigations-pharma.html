AI-Assisted Deviation Investigations in Pharma

Compliance
Published 2026-03-14 • Reviewed 2026-11-26 • 3 min read
Deviations CAPA Root Cause Analysis GMP Compliance Predictive Analytics

AI can help investigators find related records, build a timeline, or draft a list of questions. It cannot determine the cause of a deviation or decide product impact without evidence and human review.

In plain words: AI may help find clues. Investigators check the records and decide what happened.

Investigation responsibilities

For drug products under 21 CFR Part 211, section 211.100 requires written production and process-control procedures and documentation of deviations. Section 211.192 requires thorough investigation of unexplained discrepancies and failures to meet specifications. ICH Q10 describes structured investigations and CAPA within the pharmaceutical quality system. The investigation depth should fit the nature and risk of the event.

Tasks AI may support

  • Search approved deviation, batch, maintenance, laboratory, and change-control records.
  • Draft a timeline with links to the original entries.
  • Suggest possible evidence gaps or questions for the team.
  • Group similar descriptions for review without claiming a shared cause.
  • Summarize investigation status and open actions for an authorized reviewer.

AI can confuse events that sound similar, miss records, or create unsupported connections. Treat each output as a candidate to verify.

Illustrative example

Illustrative only; this is not a real company or reported result. A team investigates an unexpected process pause. AI finds a similar event from another line and points to a maintenance note. The investigator checks equipment identifiers and confirms the second event involved a different component. Engineering reviews the relevant asset history, while quality assesses product impact under procedure. AI helped search; the team assessed the evidence.

A controlled workflow

  1. Define the event, scope, and records that may be relevant.
  2. Use approved data and require source links for claims.
  3. Verify details against original records and separate hypotheses from facts.
  4. Follow procedures for investigation, product-impact assessment, and escalation.
  5. Document conclusion, uncertainty, actions, approvals, and effectiveness checks.
  6. Control changes to prompts, models, data connections, and access.

Key takeaway

AI may reduce time spent searching for records. It cannot replace a thorough investigation, product-impact assessment, or authorized quality decision. Keep the source evidence and human rationale in the controlled record.

Primary sources
