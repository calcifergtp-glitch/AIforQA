AI for Change Control Impact Assessments

Compliance
Published 2026-05-31 • Reviewed 2026-11-26 • 3 min read
Change Control Risk Assessment Quality Management System GMP CAPA Compliance

Change control helps an organization understand and manage proposed changes to products, processes, equipment, procedures, and systems. AI can help find related records and draft review questions. It cannot decide by itself whether a change is acceptable or which approvals are required.

In plain words: AI can help gather the paperwork. The right experts decide what the change could affect and whether it may proceed.

What a change-impact review should do

ICH Q10 describes a change-management system that uses quality risk management, expert review, and post-implementation evaluation to help prevent unintended effects on product quality. The review should consider the change’s scope, affected records and processes, technical justification, required approvals, and how success will be checked. Applicable regulatory filing requirements also depend on the product and region.

Where AI may help

  • Search approved procedures, deviations, CAPAs, equipment records, validation documents, and prior changes.
  • Compare document versions and flag differences for an owner to verify.
  • Prepare a draft checklist of functions, records, and subject-matter experts to consider.
  • Summarize evidence with source links and identify unanswered questions.
  • Help track approved actions, owners, and due dates.

A document match is a lead, not proof that the change affects that system. A missing search result does not show that no impact exists.

Keep risk and approval decisions with the team

Do not let AI assign a final risk category, decide whether regulatory notification is needed, approve a change, or confirm that the change worked. Qualified experts must assess technical and quality impact using the approved change-control process. Record assumptions, unresolved questions, rationale, approvals, and any follow-up actions.

Illustrative example

Illustrative only; this is not a real company or reported result. A team plans to replace a room pressure sensor. AI finds maintenance records, a calibration procedure, and a qualification protocol that mention the sensor. Engineering checks the tags and confirms which documents apply. Quality and validation assess the change, determine the required checks, and approve the plan through change control. AI helped find candidates; the team determined impact.

A controlled workflow

  1. Define the change, reason, boundaries, and affected product or process.
  2. Use AI to locate candidate records, with links to controlled sources.
  3. Ask the responsible experts to verify each affected area and identify gaps.
  4. Complete the risk assessment, approvals, and implementation plan under procedure.
  5. After implementation, document checks that the change achieved its goal without adverse impact.
  6. Update the assessment if the scope, system, evidence, or implementation changes.

Key takeaway

AI can speed up searching and prepare a clearer review. The change owner and qualified experts remain responsible for impact assessment, approval, required regulatory evaluation, and post-change checks.
