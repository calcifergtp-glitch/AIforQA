AI in GMP Document Review and Approval Workflows

Compliance
2026-05-31 • 3 min read
GMP Quality Management System Change Control Compliance Data Integrity SOP Management

AI can help with the reading, not the approval

Controlled GMP documents carry instructions people rely on. AI may help compare two revisions, find inconsistent terms, flag missing sections, or draft plain-language summaries. These are review aids. The organization still needs its approved process for writing, checking, approving, issuing, and retiring controlled documents.

Start with the document and the rule

Different records have different requirements. Written production procedures must be approved and followed; batch records and laboratory records have their own content and review requirements. Define which document types the AI may read or draft, and whether its output becomes part of a regulated record.

Keep drafts separate from effective instructions. Never let an AI-generated change become active until the required people have reviewed and approved it through document control. Preserve the approved version and its history.

Useful checks for AI-assisted review

  • Compare the proposed revision with the current approved version and list each meaningful change.
  • Flag missing references, undefined terms, mismatched equipment names, or conflicting steps.
  • Link each finding to the exact text or record that supports it.
  • Ask a subject-matter expert to decide whether the suggested change is correct and safe.
  • Check final formatting, effective date, training impact, and distribution in the controlled system.

Test the workflow with examples that include a real issue, a harmless difference, and a tricky case where the tool should not guess. Confirm it does not invent a requirement, alter a number, or confuse a draft with an effective version.

Keep evidence and responsibility clear

Define who can use the tool, what documents may be sent to it, how inputs and outputs are handled, and what must be retained. If the tool is part of an electronic record workflow, assess applicable validation, access, audit trail, and retention controls. A vendor’s marketing statement does not replace this assessment.

Key takeaway

Let AI find differences and organize review comments. Keep the approved document, final wording, effective date, and approval in the established controlled process.
