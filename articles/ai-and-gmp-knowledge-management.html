What GMP knowledge management means

People build useful knowledge from development, manufacturing, testing, investigations, and experience with a product. ICH Q10 describes knowledge management as a systematic way to acquire, analyze, store, and share information about products and processes. It supports the pharmaceutical quality system across the product lifecycle.

AI can help staff find relevant procedures, past investigations, technical reports, or training material. It can also surface outdated instructions or combine records that do not belong together. The answer is only as trustworthy as its sources and the way those sources are controlled.

Build the knowledge collection carefully

  • Use approved, current documents and label their owner, version, effective date, and status.
  • Keep superseded material separate so a search does not present an old instruction as current.
  • Limit access to confidential, personal, or restricted records according to company policy.
  • Ask the system to show the exact source passages behind an answer.
  • Route unclear or conflicting material to a qualified subject-matter expert.

A useful answer should point staff back to the source document. A generated summary is not automatically an approved procedure, training record, or quality decision. Keep updates to controlled knowledge in the existing review and approval process.

Measure whether it helps

Try realistic questions from new and experienced staff. Include cases where the answer is in one source, split across several sources, absent, or contradicted by an older document. Check whether the tool finds the right version, cites the right passages, and says when it cannot answer.

Watch for stale links, missing documents, misleading summaries, and recurring questions that may reveal a training or procedure gap. The objective is to make reliable knowledge easier to find, not to remove expert judgment.

Key takeaway

AI can make controlled knowledge easier to search. People still own the content, decide what is current, and approve any change to GMP instructions.
